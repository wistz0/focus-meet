# Privacy notes

Meet Focus stores aggregate activity totals in Chrome local storage. It does not transmit data to a server.

Collected while the user has explicitly enabled tracking:
- Aggregate time classified as active or idle while a Google Meet tab is active.
- Count of throttled mouse-movement events on Google Meet pages.

Not collected:
- Webcam or microphone media.
- Screenshots, page text, meeting chat, participant lists, or meeting recordings.
- Exact cursor coordinates, keystrokes, browsing history outside Meet, or pupil/gaze estimates.

Users can stop tracking from the extension popup and reset the displayed totals. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension data according to Chrome's behavior.

Before deploying in a school, have the institution review applicable privacy rules and provide students with clear notice and a meaningful choice.
